05. Core Engine

Model Training & Architecture

Hybrid Recommendation System

It combines Time Aware Popularity Model, User and Item Collaborative Filtering Model and content-based filtering techniques to recommend personalized products to users.

  • 50% weight is assigned to Content-based model.
  • 40% weight is assigned to Item-based Collaborative Filtering Model.
  • 20% weight is equally assigned to Time Aware Popularity Model and User-based Collaborative Filtering Model.
