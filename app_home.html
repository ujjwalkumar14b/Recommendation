05. Core Engine

Model Training & Architecture

HYBRID MODEL Content + Collaborative

Hybrid Recommendation System

Combines Neural Collaborative Filtering (NCF) with content-based feature matching to construct a highly expressive representation of user-item preferences.

  • Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP): Captures intricate non-linear relationships between customer historical embeddings and product features.
  • Data Sparsity Mitigation: Minimizes cold-start penalties by falling back to content features when user history is sparse.
  • High Personalization: Generates unique ranked item lists tailored specifically to individual interaction profiles.
