05. Model Architecture & Training

Hybrid Recommendation System

Delivers smarter recommendations by blending collaborative and content-based filtering models.

  • Combines collaborative & content features
  • Maximizes recommendation accuracy
  • Reduces data sparsity limitations
  • Highly diverse personalized suggestions
User Behavior + Product Content → Hybrid Engine
Popularity-Based Filtering

Recommends top-trending and globally popular products when user interaction data is unavailable.

  • Loved by most customers globally
  • Solves cold-start problems for new users
  • Based on purchase frequency metrics
  • Fast and lightweight computation
Purchase Frequency → Product Ranking → Top Recommendations
User-Based Collaborative Filtering

Identifies users with similar purchasing histories and recommends products preferred by similar users.

  • Clusters users with similar tastes
  • Leverages historical purchase patterns
  • Personalized without item metadata
  • Adapts dynamically as behavior shifts
User → Similar Users → Recommended Products
Item-Based Collaborative Filtering

Discovers products that are frequently bought together across customer transactions.

  • Finds closely related catalog items
  • High stability for large user bases
  • Ideal for "Frequently Bought Together"
  • Scalable item matrix computation
Purchased Item → Similar Items → Recommendations
Content-Based Filtering

Matches product metadata, titles and descriptions using feature extraction and similarity techniques.

  • Compares rich product characteristics
  • Independent of other users' actions
  • Effective for niche product discovery
  • Transparent and explainable
Product Features → Feature Extraction → Similarity → Recommendations
Recommendation Strategy Summary
Fast & Simple

Popularity-based recommendations provide a lightweight solution for cold-start scenarios.

Highly Personalized

Collaborative filtering adapts recommendations based on customer behavior and similar users.

Best Overall Balance

The Hybrid system combines multiple signals to achieve a strong balance between accuracy and personalization.
